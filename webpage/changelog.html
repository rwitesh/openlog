Changelog

1.3.9

24 September 2026

  • [Fixed] App lock can no longer lock you out of a device with no screen lock set, and turns itself back on once you add one.
  • [Improved] The font picker now shows a shorter list of readable fonts, and its lists scroll properly under the search bar.
  • [Improved] Internal fixes and cleanup for a faster, more reliable app.

1.3.8

23 September 2026

  • [Improved] Importing a backup now clearly explains when it will replace existing local entries and media, and asks for confirmation first.
  • [Fixed] Backup imports now validate archive contents, file limits, and media references before replacing local content.
  • [Improved] Backup import and export messages now use clearer, more consistent language.

1.3.7

19 September 2026

  • [Fixed] Fixed a rare issue where editing an entry could remove its photos, voice notes, or attached files after an app update.
  • [Improved] Photos, recordings, and attachments now stay reliably attached to your entries through every update, and open without trouble on any device.

1.3.6

19 September 2026

  • [Improved] Restoring a backup is now instant — your timeline updates immediately without needing to restart the app.
  • [Improved] Backups open faster and handle incomplete or corrupted files more reliably.

1.3.5

19 September 2026

  • [Improved] The timeline's vertical line now extends to the end of your last entry, and the settings row for timeline preferences is labeled "Timeline".
  • [Improved] OpenLog now asks for notification permission once, shortly after you reach your timeline, instead of during a backup — and it never asks again once you've chosen.
  • [Improved] Your app lock setting now belongs to each device, so restoring a backup never turns the lock on or off on another device.

1.3.4

19 September 2026

  • [Fixed] Backups now recover more safely if a restore is interrupted, keeping your existing timeline and media protected.
  • [Fixed] OpenLog now catches more damaged or incomplete backups before they can affect your timeline.
  • [Improved] Search continues to work reliably after restoring a backup.

1.3.3

16 September 2026

  • [Added] Create reusable, color-coded tags and add them to entries to make your timeline easier to browse and search.
  • [Fixed] Backups and restores now handle interruptions, cancellation, and unexpected file issues more safely, keeping existing timeline content recoverable.
  • [Fixed] Restores now check available device space more accurately before they begin.
  • [Improved] Backup and restore status messages are clearer and calmer.
  • [Improved] Existing timelines now move forward safely when OpenLog updates its local data structure.

1.3.2

12 September 2026

  • [Added] A link to this changelog is now available in About Settings.
  • [Improved] Documents and other file attachments now open directly in a compatible app on your device.
  • [Improved] The welcome flow is clearer and keeps the next step in view while you get started.

1.3.1

12 September 2026

  • [Fixed] Restoring a backup now recovers safely if the app is interrupted, keeping your existing timeline and media recoverable.
  • [Fixed] Backup imports now reject malformed or suspicious archives before they can replace your data.
  • [Fixed] Large timelines load more reliably as you move through older entries.
  • [Fixed] Removing media from an entry now keeps the entry and its stored files in sync, even if an update is interrupted.
  • [Improved] Your local profile name is now shown in Settings when one is available.
  • [Improved] Permission requests for backup progress are now limited to backup actions.

1.3.0

6 September 2026

  • [Added] Progress while backing up or restoring, with the option to cancel.
  • [Added] Choose where to save before an export starts.
  • [Added] Preview what's inside a backup before you restore it.
  • [Added] Tap + on any day in the calendar to add an entry for that date, not just today.
  • [Fixed] Backing up a full timeline no longer crashes the app, even with lots of photos, recordings, and files.
  • [Fixed] A corrupted backup file no longer deletes what's already on your device.
  • [Fixed] Unsaved writing is kept while the app is locked.
  • [Fixed] Entries opened from search no longer show up blank.
  • [Fixed] Removing an attachment from an entry now frees storage on your device.
  • [Fixed] Editing photos on Android no longer creates duplicate copies.
  • [Improved] Only one voice memo plays at a time. Starting another pauses the first.
  • [Improved] You now get one notification when a backup finishes, not two.
