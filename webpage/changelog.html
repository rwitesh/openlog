Changelog

1.3.2

12 September 2026

  • [Added] A link to this changelog is now available in About Settings.

1.3.1

12 September 2026

  • [Fixed] Restoring a backup now recovers safely if the app is interrupted, keeping your existing timeline and media recoverable.
  • [Fixed] Backup imports now reject malformed or suspicious archives before they can replace your data.
  • [Fixed] Large timelines load more reliably as you move through older entries.
  • [Fixed] Removing media from an entry now keeps the entry and its stored files in sync, even if an update is interrupted.
  • [Improved] Your local profile name is now shown in Settings when one is available.
  • [Improved] Permission requests for backup progress are now limited to backup actions.

1.3.0

6 September 2026

  • [Added] Progress while backing up or restoring, with the option to cancel.
  • [Added] Choose where to save before an export starts.
  • [Added] Preview what's inside a backup before you restore it.
  • [Added] Tap + on any day in the calendar to add an entry for that date, not just today.
  • [Fixed] Backing up a full timeline no longer crashes the app, even with lots of photos, recordings, and files.
  • [Fixed] A corrupted backup file no longer deletes what's already on your device.
  • [Fixed] Unsaved writing is kept while the app is locked.
  • [Fixed] Entries opened from search no longer show up blank.
  • [Fixed] Removing an attachment from an entry now frees storage on your device.
  • [Fixed] Editing photos on Android no longer creates duplicate copies.
  • [Improved] Only one voice memo plays at a time. Starting another pauses the first.
  • [Improved] You now get one notification when a backup finishes, not two.

  • [Improved] OpenLog now runs on more Android phones, including older devices.
  • [Improved] Smoother startup performance.
  • [Fixed] Under-the-hood reliability fixes.
