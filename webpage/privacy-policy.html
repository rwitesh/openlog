Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

OpenLog is an open-source, non-commercial Android app maintained by Rwitesh Bera. This policy covers the app and public website.

Information stored on your device

OpenLog does not require an account or provide cloud sync. It stores your timeline on your device so you can create, search, and view entries. Stored information includes entry text, dates, tags, locations, photos, recordings, attachments, your optional profile name, app preferences, and unsaved drafts.

Some photos stay as original files. They can contain metadata such as capture time or location. OpenLog stores selected media in app storage.

Analytics and error reports

OpenLog uses PostHog for analytics and error reports in the Android app. Events record screen names, app activity, entry actions, media counts, recording duration, backup counts and size, location use, app-lock changes, and notification choices. The app does not ask for analytics consent or provide an in-app way to turn analytics off.

PostHog receives a generated installation ID, app and device details, and the IP address of each request. These details include app version, device model, operating system, language, and time zone. The ID does not contain your name, but it lets PostHog group activity from one installation. The IP address can suggest an approximate region. PostHog processes analytics in the United States.

Regular usage events do not include your profile name, email address, entry text, photos, recordings, attachment names, or exact location coordinates. Error reports can include technical details.

PostHog describes its data handling in its privacy policy.

Location

When you choose to add or refresh a location, Android gets your current or recent location and uses its geocoding service to return a place label. That service can receive your coordinates. OpenLog saves the coordinates and place label with your entry or draft. The app does not track your location in the background. Analytics record location use, not your coordinates.

Fonts and website

If you choose an optional font, OpenLog downloads it from the jsDelivr CDN. The request includes the font file and standard connection information, such as your IP address. It does not include entry content. The app keeps the font on your device until you remove it or remove the app.

GitHub Pages hosts the OpenLog website. The site uses system fonts and has no analytics or cookie code. GitHub receives normal page request information, such as your IP address. GitHub explains its data handling in its privacy statement . The site also links to external services. Their privacy policies apply when you open those links.

Permissions and notifications

  • OpenLog requests photo access when you choose an image.
  • It requests microphone access when you start a recording.
  • It requests foreground location when you choose to add or refresh a place.
  • The system file picker lets you choose attachments and backups.

OpenLog asks for notification permission shortly after the timeline opens. If you allow it, the app shows local backup and import updates. A completion notice can show an entry count and backup size. Your device settings control whether these notices appear on the lock screen. OpenLog does not send remote push notifications.

Backups and sharing

An exported backup contains your entries, tags, locations, profile name, preferences, attachments, and media. OpenLog compresses the archive but does not encrypt it. You choose where to save or share it. The chosen folder, service, or app receives the copy.

Android system backup can copy some app preferences. OpenLog includes only its preferences in that backup, and it leaves out the app lock setting. Your timeline, photos, recordings, and drafts are never part of it. Your device settings control whether the copy happens. When you share a photo or open a file in another app, that app receives the selected file.

Retention and deletion

Saved entries and media stay on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Unsaved drafts stay until you save or discard them. Delete all entries removes entries, tags, and drafts. It also tries to remove media that no remaining entry uses. It does not remove your profile name or preferences.

Exported backups, shared files, and system backup copies remain until you or their storage provider deletes them. The app does not promise secure erasure of deleted database data.

Security

The optional app lock uses your device's biometric or passcode prompt. It does not encrypt entries, media, drafts, or backup files. Protection also depends on your device and its settings.

Age and privacy requests

OpenLog does not ask for your age or limit use by age.

Privacy rights depend on where you live. You can send privacy requests to the contact below. OpenLog has no account and keeps no central copy of your timeline.

Contact and changes
