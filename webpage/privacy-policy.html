OpenLog

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

OpenLog is a writing app made by Rwitesh Bera. Use it to keep a journal, take notes and todos, plan goals, and save your photographs and the stories you want to keep. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Everything stays on your device

Your data belongs to you. Your journals, notes, todos, plans, photos, voice notes, and settings are stored on your device. There is no account and no login. Your writing is not sent to us or to any server.

Analytics

We record a small number of anonymous events through PostHog so we can improve the app. For example, we note which screens open, how long the app runs, and when it crashes.

These events include your device model, app version, and a random ID the app generates. They never include your name, email, writing, photos, voice notes, or location. We cannot use them to identify you. Events are kept only as long as needed to understand app usage.

Permissions

The app asks for permission only when you use a feature, and only with your consent:

  • Photo library: to attach photos to your posts, and to save a photo back to your library when you choose to.
  • Microphone: to record a voice note on a post.
  • Location: to add where you are to a post. You choose this each time, and you can say no.
  • Notifications: to show reminders you set.

Everything you create stays on your device.

Export and import

You can export a backup of your data to a file you choose, and import it back on any device. These backups are handled by you alone. We never receive them.

What we never do

  • We never read or share your writing.
  • We never sell your data.
  • We never show ads or track you for advertising.

Third-party tools

  • PostHog: anonymous events. See the PostHog privacy policy.
  • Google: as with any Android app, Google may collect crash and performance data under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your device. Delete a post in the app to remove it. Uninstalling the app removes all of your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access or delete it. The app holds your writing only on your device, and our analytics are anonymous. If you have any request or question, write to us and we will help.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13. Anyone else can use it. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Any change will carry a new date at the top of this page.
